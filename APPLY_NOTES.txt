Signal Sniper consistency fix

Fixes included:
- TP strings such as "1:3 QUICKKK" are sanitized to the executable target "1:3".
- Shared RR parser accepts provider chatter after a valid RR token.
- Management fills no longer masquerade as new trade executions.
- Successful SL/TP/breakeven updates are silent in Telegram; only real entry fills, close-all results, and failures are surfaced.
- Submitted/intermediate notification events are suppressed to prevent message spam.
- MT5 health snapshots include open position tickets on Mac and Windows.
- Listener reconciles stale in-memory trades against MT5 and clears them after the broker position disappears.
- CLOSE_ALL completion clears the local active trade immediately.
- Dashboard TP prefers calculated/final numeric MT5 TP instead of the raw provider string.
- Existing fixes retained: short SS MT5 comments, filling-mode negotiation, account-wide close all, NO_CHANGES success, default RR 1:1, default lot 0.01.

Apply by replacing the matching files in ~/Desktop/signal-sniper-main, then restart the bot.
